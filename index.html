⚙ 怎样使用Jinsi ya kutumiaHow to use

  1. 整个「SAY-AI-Lab-汉语」文件夹必须放在「中文乐园」文件夹里面，页面才能播放本地视频、课本录音和真题。复制到实验室电脑时，复制整个「中文乐园」。Folda nzima 'SAY-AI-Lab-汉语' lazima ikae ndani ya folda '中文乐园' ili kurasa zicheze video, sauti za vitabu na mitihani halisi. Unaponakili kwenye kompyuta za maabara, nakili '中文乐园' yote.Keep this folder inside 中文乐园 so pages can reach the local videos, textbook audio and past papers.
  2. 读音：有录音的词句（audio 文件夹，上周资源包的 2,893 段合成录音）先播放录音；其余用本机的中文语音。iPad/电脑没有中文语音时，在「设置」里看安装方法。所有录音都是合成语音，不能代替老师示范。Matamshi: maneno yenye rekodi (folda audio, rekodi 2,893 za sauti bandia) yanachezwa kwanza; mengine yanatumia sauti ya Kichina ya kifaa. Rekodi zote ni sauti bandia — hazichukui nafasi ya mwalimu.Audio: recorded clips (2,893 synthetic clips) first, otherwise the device's Chinese voice. Synthetic, not a substitute for the teacher's model.
  3. 笔顺：1,948 个常用字的笔顺数据在 hanzi 文件夹里，离线可用；少见字联网时自动下载。Mpangilio wa mistari: data ya herufi 1,948 iko kwenye folda hanzi, bila mtandao.Stroke data for 1,948 characters is in the hanzi folder, offline.
  4. 学生用学号登记（例如 F2-07），不写名字。成绩保存在这台设备的浏览器里；老师在「进度」页导出 JSON/CSV，再在老师的电脑上导入合并。Wanafunzi wanajisajili kwa namba (k.m. F2-07), si jina. Alama zinahifadhiwa kwenye kivinjari cha kifaa hiki; mwalimu anahamisha JSON/CSV na kuziingiza kwenye kompyuta yake.Learners use codes, never names. Scores stay in this browser; teachers export and merge.
  5. AI 老师：写作题有「AI 老师批改」按钮。老师在「设置」里填 API key 后联网使用；没有 key 时按范文和评分要点自查。AI 生成或批改的内容，发给学生前必须由老师检查。Mwalimu AI: maswali ya kuandika yana kitufe cha AI. Mwalimu aweke API key kwenye Mipangilio; bila key, jisahihishe kwa mfano na vigezo. Maudhui ya AI yakaguliwe na mwalimu kwanza.AI coach needs a teacher's API key; otherwise self-check with the model and rubric. Teachers check all AI output first.
  6. HSK 版本：内容按 HSK 2.0（1–6 级）编写，与你的教材和真题一致；HSK 3.0（9 级）大纲已公布，但 2026 年的常规考试仍用 HSK 2.0，报名前向考点确认。Toleo la HSK: maudhui yanafuata HSK 2.0 (viwango 1–6), sawa na vitabu na mitihani yako. Mtaala wa HSK 3.0 umetangazwa, lakini mitihani ya kawaida ya 2026 bado ni HSK 2.0 — thibitisha na kituo cha mtihani.Content follows HSK 2.0, matching your books and past papers; regular 2026 tests still use HSK 2.0 — confirm with your test centre.
