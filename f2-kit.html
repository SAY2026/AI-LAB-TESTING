Jinsi ya kutumia kwenye kompyuta au iPad · How to use on a laptop or iPad · 在电脑或 iPad 上怎么用
  1. Mwanafunzi anasoma swali kwenye skrini na anaandika majibu kwenye karatasi: namba yake juu, kisha 1. B, 2. A …
    The student reads the questions on the screen and writes the answers on paper: the code at the top, then 1. B, 2. A …
    学生看屏幕上的题，答案写在纸上：最上面写编号，然后 1. B、2. A……
  2. Namba witiri (K01, K03 …) zinaanza na Fomu A; namba shufwa (K02, K04 …) zinaanza na Fomu B. Baada ya mtihani mfupi, kila mmoja anafanya fomu nyingine.
    Odd codes start with Form A; even codes start with Form B. After the short mock, everyone takes the other form.
    单号先做 A 卷，双号先做 B 卷；模考之后各做另一套。
  3. Kila ukurasa una kitufe cha Chapisha · Print. Fungua ukurasa huu mara moja kwenye kila kifaa ukiwa na intaneti; baadaye kurasa zinafunguka bila intaneti.
    Every page has a Print button. Open this page once on every device while online; after that the pages open without internet.
    每页都有打印按钮。趁有网时在每台设备上先打开本页一次，之后断网也能打开各页。
Darasa lipi litumie nini? · Which class uses what? · 哪个班用什么？
  1. Ngazi ya 1 · Level 1 · 基础: Kijitabu cha 1 → Mtihani mfupi (dakika 60). For classes that are still learning the basic words and question types. 还在打基础的班：复习册 1 → 模考半卷。
